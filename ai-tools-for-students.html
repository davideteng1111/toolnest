Students can use AI tools to save time, understand difficult topics, organize ideas and improve their learning process. The best tool depends on what you are trying to accomplish.

How students should use AI

AI should be used as a learning assistant rather than simply copying answers. Ask AI to explain difficult concepts, create practice questions, review your work and help you understand mistakes.
